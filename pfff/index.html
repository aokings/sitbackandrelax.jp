Rules

A card game that secretly turns into Old Maid.

Free to play. No downloads. No signups.

pfff! is a 4-player strategy game where you pair cards, hide your best ones behind Gates ⛪️, and blast open your rivals' defenses with Gust attacks 🌀. Then the deck runs out — and suddenly you're playing Old Maid with a Vampire 🧛 nobody wants.

How it works
  • Draw → Pair → Shelter → Attack → Survive. Match pairs to score. Shelter high-value cards behind Gates so nobody can steal them.
  • Spend Gust cards to rip open opponents' Gates — but you might need that Gust to defend yourself later.
  • When the deck runs out, it's Old Maid time. Don't be the one holding the unpaired card.
