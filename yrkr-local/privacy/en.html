Privacy Policy

Yarikuri Local (yrkr.local)

Yarikuri Local ("the App") is an app designed for managing children's pocket money. This policy explains how we handle your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information.

  • No account registration is required
  • All data such as names and amounts is stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to any server

2. iCloud Backup

The App provides automatic backup using iCloud Documents.

  • Backup data is stored in your own iCloud storage, linked to your Apple ID
  • The developer cannot access this data
  • Backups can be disabled at any time from the app's settings

3. Camera Usage

The App uses the camera to scan QR codes.

  • Images captured by the camera are used solely for QR code analysis and are neither saved nor transmitted

4. Data Sharing via QR Codes

The App uses QR codes to share data between parent and child devices.

  • QR codes contain transaction history (amounts, reasons, dates)
  • QR codes are displayed and scanned directly between devices, without using the internet

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be used by children. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from children. All data remains on the device.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as needed. If significant changes are made, we will notify you within the app.
